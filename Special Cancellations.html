What are Special Cancellations?

Special cancellations are unique commemorative postmarks applied to mail and philatelic covers to honor specific historic anniversaries, national events, or UNESCO World Heritage monuments. Hand-carved and available only for limited duration at designated General Post Offices (GPOs), these cancellations represent treasured collectors' artifacts. Registered National Philately Deposit Account (PDA) holders can request postal officers to apply these special postmarks on their purchased items and First Day Covers before dispatch.
